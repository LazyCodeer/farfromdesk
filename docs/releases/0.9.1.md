# FarFromDesk 0.9.1

## Changes

- Control your Mac from your phone. Send "control my screen" to FarFromDesk, open the link it sends, send back the code the page shows, then enter your remote password. You see and use your whole Mac in your phone browser: tap, scroll, type, use shortcut keys, switch desktops and open Mission Control.
- The connection goes straight between your phone and your Mac, encrypted with WebRTC (DTLS). Both sides check each other's key fingerprint, which travels only in the codes you send in your own chat. Links work once and expire after 5 minutes, and every connection needs your remote password.
- Unlock your Mac from your phone. When your Mac is locked, the lock screen stays visible and Type hides what you enter. Your Mac password goes straight into your Mac and is never stored.
- Type with your voice. Tap the mic and speak, using the voice setup you already chose in FarFromDesk. Voice typing is off while your Mac is locked.
- Safer when you step away. After 30 seconds without a touch, a notice at the top asks if you are still there, and the session closes 15 seconds later if not. Turning your phone screen off, reloading or closing the page pauses the session and hides your Mac's screen. Your remote password picks it back up, and a paused session closes after 10 minutes.
- If the connection drops, your Mac sends a new link to your chat. Within 10 minutes it reconnects without the password, unless the session was paused.
- Adds a preview of phone access on the same Wi-Fi from the desktop app, which also sets the remote password used by phone control.
- Sessions can offer choices in their replies. On Telegram they appear as buttons. On WhatsApp they appear as a numbered list; reply to that message with a number.
- Windows can now install updates from inside the app, with a safe shutdown of the background service before installing.

## Requirements and limitations

Phone control needs a Mac running FarFromDesk with Accessibility and Screen Recording allowed, and a phone with a current Safari or Chrome. The phone page is served from ffd.codecylabs.com, but your screen, taps and typing never pass through it. The connection uses public STUN servers (Google and Cloudflare) to find a direct path. There is no relay, so a network that blocks direct connections in both directions cannot connect.

Windows versions through 0.9.0 need one manual download to get the in-app updater.

This build is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**.

On first open, macOS may say FarFromDesk can't be checked. Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If it is still blocked, open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**, then **Open Anyway**. Depending on your macOS version, you may need to attempt to open the app once before Open Anyway appears. Confirm these prompts yourself only if you trust the download.

Accessibility, Screen Recording, Automation or external-drive access may need granting again after an update because the ad-hoc signing identity can change.
