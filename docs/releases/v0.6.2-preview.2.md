# Sidekick for Mac — first private preview

Keep your coding assistants close, even when you step away from your desk.

## Download

Under **Assets**, choose **arm64** for Apple Silicon or **x86_64** for Intel. Download the DMG and drag **Sidekick.app** into Applications, or download the ZIP and unzip the same app bundle. Each architecture has its own checksum file and manifest. The source-code ZIP and tarball generated by GitHub are not the app installer.

Requires a **Mac with macOS 14 or later**, a compatible signed-in Codex or Claude installation, and Python 3 for the native desktop helper. Node and the local voice transcription runtime/model are bundled. Keep your Mac online and available.

**This is a private, ad-hoc signed, non-notarized preview. macOS may block it.** It is intended for invited testers, not a frictionless public installation. Do not disable macOS security protections. A notarized public installer is still pending.

## In this preview

- Connect WhatsApp using a QR code.
- Choose Message yourself or a supported owner-only group.
- Choose Codex or Claude, Desktop or CLI, and your coordinating session.
- Select multiple coding sessions, ask for updates, and send requests.
- Transcribe supported voice notes locally on your Mac.
- Receive approved documents and inspect unfinished requests in Activity.
- Optional background and idle-sleep prevention settings.

## First steps

1. Open Sidekick and link WhatsApp from Linked devices.
2. Choose your assistant and reply destination.
3. Message it to show your sessions, select one, and ask for an update.

## Notes for testers

The Intel build is cross-compiled on Apple Silicon. Rosetta smoke checks do not replace an actual Intel Mac test; Intel hardware verification is pending.

Provider availability depends on account, app version, native permissions and session reachability. Desktop login does not automatically authenticate a CLI. Not all provider/version combinations have completed a real-phone test. Automatic in-app updates are not included; future downloads appear here.

Existing state and service identifiers retain their original names for compatibility. If you already have a local development bridge running, this app reuses it; this ZIP does not silently upgrade that running service.

**Windows and Linux apps are in the pipeline—coming soon.** There are no Windows or Linux binaries in this release.
