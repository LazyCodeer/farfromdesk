# Brand-directed taste

The owner approved dimensional illustrations, indigo/lavender Splice styling and strong editorial typography. The cart, knowledge book, control rails and conversation objects are concrete visual metaphors. Prefer this craft to default rectangular cards or a generic hub diagram.

For each campaign, use the actual brand's colours and assets. The included illustrations are indigo/lavender; choose compatible styling or draw an original scene for a materially different palette. Splice's optional exact icon belongs only to Splice AI. Other brands can use a text label until their assets are configured; never invent a logo. Do not add platform marks merely for decoration or imply unsupported integrations.

Keep one focal object or coherent still life, directional highlights, 4+ stop material gradients, visible edge thickness, contact shadows and consistent perspective. Avoid random blobs, fake dashboards, disconnected faces and shiny clutter. A rich composition needs useful form, not more objects. Original drawings can use layered vector shapes to build depth.

Use an image-led cover, varied supporting compositions and a concrete CTA. Headlines should be short and readable; explanatory copy belongs in the caption or a second slide. The scene layout reserves a 1000 × 650 drawing plane. Keep illustration text out of that plane and use the typed headline/body/footer fields for reliable fonts. Campaign headers use Bricolage Grotesque; body uses Instrument Sans. Do not claim these are every brand's official fonts.

Review: full output, phone preview, clipping, overlap, logo integrity, hierarchy, fact support, slide order, consistent terminology and whether the image explains the topic. Revise a failing slide. No em/en dashes in public copy. Photos and videos require actual corresponding tools; do not label vector art as a photograph or a video.

Cart-recovery evidence example: the Splice source describes WhatsApp reminders, voice help on the no-reply path and configured order verification. A positive reply is not a verified recovery. Do not promise native Shopify integration, immediate purchase monitoring or invented uplift metrics. Research new topics separately.
