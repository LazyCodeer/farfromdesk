How your connection is protected

  • Straight to your MacYour screen, taps and typing go directly between this phone and your Mac, never through a FarFromDesk server.
  • EncryptedEverything is encrypted with DTLS, the standard encryption built into WebRTC.
  • Keys checked on both endsBoth devices check each other's SHA-256 key fingerprint. It travels only in the codes you send in your own chat, so no other device can step in.
  • Your password, every timeEach connection needs your remote password. Links expire after 5 minutes and work once.
  • Your Mac passwordTyped on the lock screen, it goes over this connection straight into your Mac, never to any other service. FarFromDesk never stores or logs it.
